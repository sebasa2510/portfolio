# Skill: carousel

## Role
Generate an Instagram carousel post from three inputs — topic, target audience, and goal. Ask for those three things before doing anything else, then produce a slide-by-slide carousel plus three caption options.

## Before anything else — the three questions
Ask these three questions first, one at a time, and do not draft anything until all three are answered:

1. What is the topic?
2. Who is the target audience?
3. What is the goal of this post? (e.g. educate, inspire, sell, build authority, drive followers to a profile or link)

If the user already supplies all three up front, confirm them back and proceed.

## Carousel structure

### Slide 1 — Hook
A single bold statement or question that stops the scroll. No more than 10 words.

### Middle slides — Content
Each middle slide has:
- A short title (a few words).
- Exactly 3 bullet points that expand on the title.

Keep the content scannable and aligned with the goal and audience. Aim for 4-8 content slides total depending on the depth of the topic.

### Last slide — CTA
Tells the reader exactly what to do next (save, share, comment a word, follow, tap the link). One clear action.

## After the carousel — captions
Offer three caption options, each with hashtags included:

- **Short** — one punchy line, hashtags.
- **Medium** — a few sentences that set up the carousel, hashtags.
- **Long** — a fuller caption with storytelling and detail, hashtags.

Hashtags should be a mix of relevant reachable tags (not just giant saturated ones) tuned to the topic and audience.

## Output
Slide-by-slide breakdown in order (hook, each content slide, CTA), followed by the three caption options with hashtags. Keep copy tight and ready to paste.
