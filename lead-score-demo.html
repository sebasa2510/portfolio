How the scoring model works

The client specification defines the four criteria, their weights, and the grade bands. It does not define how each criterion is scored internally, so the sub-scoring rubric below is a reasonable implementation of that intent and is shown here in full rather than hidden.

CriterionWeightHow the sub-score is derived
Verified budget fit35%Stated budget cross-referenced against verified revenue. A budget at or under 15% of annual revenue scores full marks; above that the score drops and a risk flag is raised. If revenue is unknown the budget is treated as self-reported and scores lower. Missing budget scores lowest of all and forces a provisional grade.
Purchasing authority30%Derived from job title: founder / C-suite highest, then VP and Director, then Manager, then individual contributor. An unknown title is treated as a failed enrichment lookup, which caps the lead at grade C per the specification.
Immediate need25%Service-scope match plus whether a concrete operational problem was described. A clear platform or workflow request with a stated pain scores full marks; a match with no stated problem scores partially.
Timeline urgency10%A 31–90 day start earns full points as specified. Shorter starts score slightly lower, longer exploratory timelines score progressively less, and anything beyond six months is routed to the educational nurture track.

Grade bands. A ≥ 80 and only when budget, authority and need are all confirmed. B 65–79. C 50–64, or any critical data missing. D 35–49. F below 35, or any auto-disqualification rule.

Auto-disqualification. Basic social media posting, low-budget graphic design, and any non-platform request are rejected before scoring begins.
